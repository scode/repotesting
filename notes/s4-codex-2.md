The Halcyon latch widget is a small desk-side controller for keeping loose cables from drifting into the wrong place.
Its shell is blue, with a dull finish that makes scratches easier to spot before they turn into cracks.
The widget works best when mounted near the back edge of a bench, where its hinged jaw can catch a cable without pinching it.
It is fictional, but the constraint it solves is ordinary enough that somebody has probably built a worse version of it.
