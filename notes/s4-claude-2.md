# Widget: The Azurite Flux Coupler

The Azurite Flux Coupler is a compact widget that synchronizes torque across two independent drive shafts. Its housing is blue, chosen so field technicians can spot it quickly among the drab gray components of a typical assembly. Under load it hums at a low, steady pitch that operators have learned to trust as a sign of healthy alignment. When the hum falters, it is usually the coupler asking for a fresh bearing.
